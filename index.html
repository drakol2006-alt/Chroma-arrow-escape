How to Play

Easy to learn, hard to master

1 · Only the target colour moves

The banner shows the active target colour. After every successful escape, the target jumps to the next colour in the level's mixed sequence — it might go Red, then Green, then Red again. Arrows of any other colour are locked — tapping one costs a heart, even if its exit looks wide open.

2 · The exit path must be clear

An arrow slides out in the direction its head points, out through the edge of the shape. If any arrow sits in its path, it's blocked — tap a blocked arrow and the one in its way flashes red, with a ring on the exact cell where it blocks you. Bent arrows (L, U and zig-zag) still exit head-first.

3 · A different shape on every level

Level 1 is a square, Level 2 a triangle, Level 3 a circle, Level 4 a diamond — then the creative boards arrive fast: a mushroom at Level 5, an elephant at 8, a cat at 10, a horse at 15 and a maze at 20, mixed in with pentagon, hexagon, heart, star, cross, ring, lightning, arrowhead and spiral, so no two levels in a row ever look the same. The same shapes you meet in the Daily Challenge are all here in the levels too — nothing is daily-only. Every silhouette is packed edge to edge with thin winding arrows — the whole shape is filled, right out to its edges and tip — and the outline changes the exits and blocking paths. A new arrow colour also unlocks every 5 levels — Green at Level 5, Orange at 10, Purple at 15, Yellow at 20, Cyan at 25 — and stays in the pool forever.

R → G → R → B

Mixed sequence — a new target every move

Each level hides a built-in colour sequence generated together with the board, so the puzzle is always solvable. The target changes after every escape — Red → Green → Red → Blue is normal, and the same colour can appear twice in a row, but it never waits for a whole colour to be finished. Watch the NEXT dots in the banner to plan ahead.

4 · Hearts per level

Wrong colour or blocked arrow = one heart lost. Lose all your hearts and the level ends. (A claimed Daily Reward adds bonus hearts for the day.) Within a colour, free blocked arrows by removing the ones in front first.

⭐

5 · Stars, target time & moves

3 stars need a perfect board — no mistakes, no hints — finished inside the target time shown next to the clock. You also have a limited move budget: every tap counts, including wrong ones. Undo gives one move back, and running out of moves ends the level.

💡

Tools & keys

Undo reverses your last escape, Hint highlights an arrow that can escape right now, Restart resets the level. On desktop: U undo · H hint · R restart · Esc pause.
